## What I worked on

The catalog expansion grew the Task 13 seed from two to 27 published products.
It preserved the original fixtures, added 25 products with official supplied
Eazy assessments, matched product facts and offers by exact style code, and
pinned committed product-image URLs to an immutable commit.

## Problem discovered

Two products were enough to prove connected states but not enough to judge a
Feed containing ranked and repeated catalog sections. Expanding the data could
easily turn into a scraper, importer, new schema, or production write—none of
which was required to answer the immediate product question.

## Decision made

The project used one seed-only packet and kept the established schema,
triggers, RLS, grants, and Task 13 identifiers unchanged. The seed did not
create users or ratings, did not write aggregates, and did not create an admin
or import pipeline.

## Evidence captured

Pull request #50 and its merge commit record human acceptance and database
checks. The implementation commit contains the deterministic data block, while
the image commit remains reachable because the merge strategy preserved it.
The task record limits the image use to the maintainer-directed personal-study
seed context.

## Result

The local catalog became large and varied enough to evaluate Feed composition
and remeasure Browse scale. Production application remained a separate gate.
