## What I worked on

Task 16 added email/password sign-up, sign-in, sign-out, session restoration,
a minimal Account surface, and an authentication gate around Rate. It also
isolated user-scoped caches and restored the intended destination after a
successful sign-in.

## Problem discovered

Authentication changes asynchronously. A stale restore or profile response
could otherwise overwrite a newer sign-in or sign-out state, and cached
account data could survive across users. At the same time, requiring a session
for Browse would violate the product's anonymous discovery rule.

## Decision made

The app kept the catalog public and placed the boundary at user-owned actions.
Generation guards rejected stale auth work, sign-out settled local state, and
navigation returned people to the protected flow they had chosen. Recovery,
durable My Rating, and deletion stayed in later tasks.

## Evidence captured

Pull request #35 and its merge commit contain the implementation and review
history. The evidence report records automated and web verification plus a
corrected physical-iPhone checklist reported as passing. That evidence does
not claim production authentication configuration.

## Result

Eazy Review gained account state without turning sign-in into a prerequisite
for product discovery. The authenticated boundary was ready for durable,
owner-only rating writes.
