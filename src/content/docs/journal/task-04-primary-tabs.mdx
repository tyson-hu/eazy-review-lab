This article describes the first tab model;
later work changed the initial destination from Feed to Browse.

## What I worked on

Task 4 removed the sample Expo tabs and created Eazy Review's first three
destinations: Feed, Browse, and Account. It also replaced the starter modal and
route names with product-specific files and icons.

## Problem discovered

A working router still presented a generic starter application. Until the
top-level destinations matched the product vocabulary, later screens would be
attached to a navigation model that communicated the wrong priorities.

## Decision made

The application adopted three small tab surfaces and initially redirected the
root route to Feed. That initial-route choice was not permanent: the later
post-Task-12 roadmap review made Browse the default while preserving the same
three destinations.

## Evidence captured

The app-shell commit shows the deleted starter routes and the new Feed, Browse,
and Account files. The archived task plan records the required tab names and
root redirect. This is implementation evidence, not proof of the later Browse
default or current Feed behavior.

## Result

Eazy Review became recognizable at the navigation level before its catalog and
rating experiences existed. The shell was small enough to evolve without a
migration to another navigation system.
