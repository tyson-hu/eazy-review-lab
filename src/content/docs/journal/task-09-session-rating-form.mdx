This article describes a local interaction
prototype; it does not claim authentication or database persistence.

## What I worked on

Task 9 replaced the rating placeholder with inputs for the shared ten
dimensions, a live derived My Rating, an optional private note, validation,
and create-versus-edit behavior within the current application session.

## Problem discovered

The rating model needed interaction proof before security and storage were
added. A manually editable overall score would also conflict with the product
rule that My Rating is derived from the individual dimensions.

## Decision made

The form kept the ten half-step inputs as the source values and computed the
0–100 result. It supported prefilled editing and honest session-only save
feedback, while deferring login, database writes, and server-owned Community
Score updates.

## Evidence captured

The initial implementation commit records the local form and My Rating
calculation. Pull request #10 and its merge commit contain the completed route,
shared `RatingInputRow`, contract updates, and integration back into Product
Detail.

## Result

The core rating interaction could be evaluated end to end without confusing a
prototype save with durable product behavior. Its limitations became explicit
requirements for Tasks 16 and 17.
