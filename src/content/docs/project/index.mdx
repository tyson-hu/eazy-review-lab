Eazy Review is a clean product discovery and rating app. It helps people answer
whether a product is good, whether it is worth buying, how Eazy rates it, how
the community rates it, how they rated it themselves, and where they can buy it.

## Vocabulary

Use these product terms exactly:

- **Eazy Score** — the app's curated rating created by the app owner.
- **Community Score** — the average rating from normal users.
- **My Rating** — the logged-in user's own rating for the product.

## Core flow

Browse products → open product detail → compare **Eazy Score** and **Community Score** → submit or edit **My Rating**.

Browsing does not require login. Rating does.
